About Green Glow Services

Green Glow Services is a professionally managed cleaning, housekeeping, facility management and manpower solutions company headquartered in Surat, Gujarat.

We are committed to delivering high-quality support services to Government Offices, Public Sector Undertakings, Corporate Offices, Commercial Complexes, Industries, Hospitals, Educational Institutions and Residential Communities.

Our Vision

To become India's most trusted facility management and manpower solutions company.

Our Mission

  • Provide professional cleaning services.
  • Create employment opportunities.
  • Support government and private organizations.
  • Deliver customer-focused service solutions.
  • Promote safe and hygienic workplaces.
